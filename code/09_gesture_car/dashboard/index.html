        Gestures

        Each gesture is a hand pose plus what it makes the car do. Built-in ones are geometry rules you can read; taught ones learn from examples you show the camera. Taught gestures are checked first.

          Policy rules

          Hand model
          Full is about twice the work for the Pi's CPU.
          Engine
          Two MediaPipe workers take turns on frames: about twice the gesture rate. The video itself always runs at the camera's speed.
          When no gesture is seen
          S (stop) is the safe choice: the car keeps its last key otherwise.

          Add a built-in gesture

          Rules are plain geometry: which fingers are out and where the finger or thumb points. Directions are as you see them on screen.
